MILESTONE TIMELINE

6 MONTHS
  • Turns head when you call his name
  • Smiles back at you
  • Responds to sound by making sound
  • Sits without support for a short time
  • Likes social play (peek-a-boo)
12 MONTHS (1 YR)
  • Uses simple gestures (shaking head "no")
  • Pulls up to stand
  • Copies you during play
  • Responds when told "no"
  • Says "mama" and "dada"
18 MONTHS (1½ YR)
  • Plays pretend (talking on toy phone)
  • Points to interesting things
  • Uses several single words to get things
  • Walks without help
  • Looks at something when you point
2 YEARS
  • Uses 2 to 4 word phrases
  • Shows more interest in other children
  • Follows simple instructions
  • Kicks a ball
  • Points to a toy or picture when you name it
3 YEARS
  • Shows affection for playmates
  • Copies adults and playmates
  • Uses 4 to 5 word sentences
  • Climbs well
  • Plays make-believe with dolls/animals
4 YEARS
  • Follows 3 step commands
  • Hops and stands on one foot
  • Uses 5 to 6 word sentences
  • Shares and takes turn with other children
  • Draws circles and squares

VACCINES : THINGS YOU SHOULD KNOW

BCG
Bacillus Calmette-Guerin (BCG) vaccine is used against Tuberculosis (TB) which infects lungs and other parts of a baby.
Diphtheria
Diphtheria vaccine prevents Diphtheria which causes fever, sore throat, malaise, and loss of appetite.
Tetanus
Tetanus vaccine prevents Tetanus (Lockjaw) which causes muscle rigidity and convulsive spasms of muscles.
Pertussis
Pertussis vaccine prevents whooping cough. This bacteria infects the respiratory tract and spreads easily.
IPV
Inactivated Polio Vaccine (IPV) prevents Polio caused by the Poliovirus. This virus leads to paralysis.
PCV
Pneumococcal Conjugate Vaccine (PCV) protects babies against Pneumonia caused by Streptococcus pneumoniae.
Typhoid
Typhoid vaccine prevents infection causing prolonged fever, headache, nausea, and loss of appetite.
MMR
MMR vaccine protects against measles, mumps and rubella which are highly contagious childhood viral infections.
Hepatitis A
Hepatitis A vaccine prevents highly contagious short term liver infection. HAV spreads when someone ingests the virus.
Hepatitis B
Hepatitis B vaccine prevents infection which can cause life long liver infection, cirrhosis, and liver failure.
